# Review sessions and homework

Your homework progress shows how many qualifying sessions you have completed this week. For example, “0 of 2 sessions — at least 10 reviews each” means you need two sessions with at least 10 reviews in each session.

Each homework session includes at least 10 reviews. If fewer cards are ready, we add upcoming reviews, starting with your study focus. If there aren't enough different cards, some will repeat. A class with a higher review minimum receives enough reviews to meet that minimum instead.

The queue starts with cards due for review and new cards within your remaining daily allowance. Short queues are filled with already learned cards closest to their next review date. We try your selected study focus first (or the homework CardSets when no specific focus is selected), then other cards in your class deck. Filling a short queue does not increase your daily new-card allowance.

The full queue is shown in the progress counter from the beginning. Sessions can be longer than the minimum when more cards are ready. Cards marked “I forgot it” may appear again, increasing the queue during the session. Each graded appearance counts as one review, including repeats; you do not need to answer every card correctly to earn homework credit.

Finish the session to record your progress. Using “Finish Early” before reaching the required number of reviews does not earn credit. Two shorter sessions do not combine into one qualifying session.

Complete qualifying sessions on different calendar days, at least 12 hours apart. A longer session still earns one session of homework credit. Weekly homework runs from Monday through Sunday using the app's Asia/Shanghai calendar boundaries.

If your deck has no cards yet, check back after cards have been added to your class.
