# Review sessions and homework

Your homework progress shows how many qualifying sessions you have completed this week. For example, “0 of 2 sessions — at least 10 reviews each” means you need two sessions with at least 10 reviews in each session.

Each homework session includes at least 10 reviews. If fewer cards are ready, we add upcoming reviews, within your study focus. If there aren't enough different cards, some will repeat. A class with a higher review minimum receives enough reviews to meet that minimum instead.

The queue starts with cards due for review and new cards within your remaining daily allowance. Short queues are filled with already learned cards closest to their next review date. A selected study focus strictly limits the session to those CardSets, including upcoming reviews and repeats. You can re-study those cards even when none are due. With All CardSets selected, upcoming homework cards are preferred before other cards in your class deck. Filling a short queue does not increase your daily new-card allowance. If only new cards remain in your focus and that allowance is exhausted, choose “Study More New Words” to continue within the same focus. Personal new cards use priority 3; lower priority numbers are introduced first.

The full queue is shown in the progress counter from the beginning. Sessions can be longer than the minimum when more cards are ready. Cards marked “I forgot it” may appear again, increasing the queue during the session. Each graded appearance counts as one review, including repeats; you do not need to answer every card correctly to earn homework credit.

Finish the session to record your progress. Using “Finish Early” before reaching the required number of reviews does not earn credit. Two shorter sessions do not combine into one qualifying session.

Complete qualifying sessions on different calendar days, at least 12 hours apart. A longer session still earns one session of homework credit. Weekly homework runs from Monday through Sunday using the app's Asia/Shanghai calendar boundaries.

If your deck has no cards yet, check back after cards have been added to your class.

After finishing a session, the Study Focus dropdown keeps your selection. “Keep Reviewing” starts another session within that focus, even for a small set you have already studied. You can choose a different focus on the completion screen. “Keep Studying” practices only the weak cards from the session you just finished. Clicking Study in the navigation returns to the setup screen and restores the default focus.
