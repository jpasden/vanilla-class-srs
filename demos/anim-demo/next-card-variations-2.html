Grade-Direction Swipe — Trajectory Variations

Left/right still tied to grade (Again/Hard → left, Good/Easy → right), but each exit's exact trajectory (distance, rotation, arc, speed) is randomized within a range every time, so no two swipes look identical.

1. Randomized rotation only (±range)

Direction fixed by grade, distance/speed fixed, but rotation angle randomizes each time within ±(4°–14°) — simplest variation, still clearly "tossed," never the exact same spin twice.

2. Randomized vertical arc

3. Randomized speed + distance together

4. Everything randomized (rotation + arc + speed + distance)

All of the above combined — direction is the only fixed thing per grade, everything else (rotation 5°–16°, vertical drift ±25px, speed 0.24s–0.34s, distance 110%–145%) rolls fresh each swipe. Feels the most "alive"/hand-thrown.

5. Magnitude scales with grade strength

Same random-everything approach as #4, but "Again" (grade 1) and "Easy" (grade 4) throw harder/faster (more rotation, more distance, less duration) than the gentler "Hard"/"Good" (grades 2/3) — the swipe's intensity reflects how strongly you graded the card, not just its direction.
