Swipe-Away — Variations

Same "swipe off the deck" idea, different directions/speed/drama. Click each button repeatedly to cycle.

1. Baseline (swipe left)

Card flies off to the left with a slight rotation — the version you already saw.

2. Swipe right

Mirror direction. Purely a feel preference — some users associate "right" with "done/forward" more naturally (like Tinder's "like" swipe).

3. Direction tied to grade

Buttons below let you pick a grade like the real app does. "Again"/"Hard" swipe left (red/orange side), "Good"/"Easy" swipe right (green/blue side) — gives the swipe itself meaning instead of being purely decorative.

4. Fast & aggressive throw

0.2s exit, less rotation, slight upward arc as it flies — feels punchy/quick rather than graceful. Good fit if sessions have many cards and speed matters more than ceremony.

5. Drop & discard

Card drops downward and shrinks, like being discarded face-down onto a pile, instead of flying sideways. Calmer than a swipe but still has clear motion.

6. Big dramatic swipe

More rotation (20°) and a scale-down as it exits, plus the next card eases in with its own slight counter-rotation. Most "extra" of the bunch — fun once in a while, but might feel like too much for every single card.
