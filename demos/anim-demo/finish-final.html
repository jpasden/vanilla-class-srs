Final Finish Screen — Creative Round

Confetti (settles on ground) and the checkmark draw are unchanged throughout. The numbers are bigger and use "shake & settle" + "glow pop" combined, randomized accent colors, and performance-based emoji. Each variation below changes everything around that combo to make the whole screen feel like a distinct reward moment, not just an animated number. Click Replay on each — a few even change based on a (fake, randomized) performance tier so you can see the good/bad variants.

1. Baseline: big shake-glow numbers + color/emoji by performance

Just the core ask on its own: numbers ~3x bigger than before, shake+glow combined, glow color picked from the palette at random, and an emoji next to the heading that reflects accuracy (🔥 90%+, 👍 70-89%, 💪 below 70%).

2. Personal best banner

If the (randomized, fake) session beats a "previous best" accuracy, a small gold ribbon banner slides in above the stats: "New personal best!" — ties the reward to genuine progress, not just "you finished," and only appears when actually earned.

3. Streak flame counter

A flickering flame emoji + "Day streak" counter sits beside the main stats, counting up its own number on a slight delay — taps into daily-habit motivation (Duolingo-style) rather than just this one session's score.

4. Encouraging line that reacts to the actual numbers

Instead of a generic "Session Complete," the headline text itself changes based on performance — "Nailed it!" / "Solid work." / "Keep going — you'll get there." — feels like the app is actually responding to how you did, not showing the same screen regardless.

5. Mascot reaction (deck icon "looks at" your score)

The app's own Vanilla-deck mascot icon appears bigger, and tilts/bounces differently depending on performance (excited bounce for a great session, a gentler nod for an okay one) — reuses existing brand art instead of generic icons, gives the screen a "character" reacting to you.

6. Medal/badge drop

A small circular medal (🥇/🥈/🥉 by accuracy tier) drops in from above and lands with a bounce above the stats, like an actual achievement badge — most "gamified" option, clearest sense of tiered reward.

7. Receipt-style line-item reveal

Instead of two stat blocks appearing at once, results print out one line at a time like a receipt ("Cards reviewed... 12", "Correct on first try... 9", "Accuracy... 83%"), each sliding in just after the last — builds a little suspense/rhythm before the final number lands, and surfaces more detail than just two numbers.

8. Filling progress ring around the accuracy number

Accuracy is shown as a circular ring that fills clockwise from 0 to its final value (instead of a plain stat block) with the percentage glowing/popping in the center once the ring finishes — accuracy gets visual weight as the "headline" stat, cards-reviewed becomes a smaller secondary line below.

9. Rubber-stamp "PASSED" / tier stamp

A bordered text stamp (like a customs/passport stamp) slams down at an angle onto the screen — "GREAT JOB" / "WELL DONE" / "KEEP PRACTICING" depending on accuracy tier — punchy, single decisive beat rather than a string of small animations.

10. Everything combined (the "full reward" screen)

Mascot reaction + medal + reactive headline + glow numbers + confetti, all at once — the most elaborate option. Best reserved for genuinely good sessions (e.g. only show the full version above some accuracy threshold, with a quieter version otherwise) rather than firing at full intensity every time.
