Design Style Guide — living reference for all UI decisions

1. Aesthetic

The project is called Vanilla. Every design decision should reflect this name — vanilla is the foundation, the default, the warm and welcoming base that everything else is built on.

Think ice cream parlor — warm, friendly, nostalgic, and approachable. Soft pastels, rounded shapes, and the feeling of a sunny afternoon with a scoop in hand. Playful but not chaotic. Never cold, corporate, or sharp-edged.

16. Do / Don't

✓ Do

  • Use vanilla or cornsilk for all large surfaces
  • Round everything — pill buttons, 1rem cards
  • Warm-toned shadows only
  • Fredoka for all headings, nav, buttons
  • Nunito for all body copy and form text
  • Strawberry for CTAs, links, and danger actions
  • Generous whitespace — let the pastels breathe

✗ Don't

  • Use #ffffff or #000000 anywhere
  • Use cold blues, grays, or dark backgrounds
  • Use square corners on cards or buttons
  • Use Inter, Roboto, Arial, or system-ui
  • Use purple gradients or "tech startup" styles
  • Use heavy drop shadows or dramatic contrast
  • Use Pacifico for anything smaller than hero text
